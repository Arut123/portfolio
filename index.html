01 About IIT Roorkee · Integrated MSc Physics
2020–2025

I started out doing physics, not software — chasing gravitational-wave glitches through the Linear Chirp Transform, wiring log-periodic dipole antennas for the SKA at Raman Research Institute, and tracking coronal mass ejections with LSTMs at ARIES. That instrumentation-first, signal-first way of thinking is still how I approach every ML problem: understand the noise before you trust the model.

Today that's evolved into building generative and agentic AI systems — neural-operator surrogates for ultrasonic non-destructive testing at IIT Madras, offline quantized LLM pipelines for enterprise document auditing, and statistical fraud/AML models trained on millions of real banking transactions. I like problems where the data is messy, the domain is unfamiliar, and the model has to actually hold up in production.
